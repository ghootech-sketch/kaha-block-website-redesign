Source basis: Google Ads Search Terms report, 1–26 October 2026. The report contains 269 search-term rows; duplicates in the source are meaningful demand observations.

## Principle

Real search language is treated as demand evidence, not pasted verbatim across every page. Each commercial intent has one primary URL to reduce keyword cannibalization.

## Search-language variants used naturally

- paving block / paving blok / conblock / konblok
- pabrik / produsen / supplier
- jual / beli / toko
- terdekat
- jasa pasang / pemasangan / tukang paving / borongan / kontraktor
- harga per m² / meter persegi / satuan / per biji

These variants are distributed according to user intent. They must not be repeated mechanically.

## Terms not targeted on commercial pages

### Competitor / third-party brand terms
Cisangkan, Masterblock, Mutiara, Asia Con, Hollywood, PD Conblock Nusantara, UD Pusaka.

### Used / second-hand intent
paving bekas, conblock bekas, paver block second hand.

### Out-of-coverage locations
Surabaya, Cimahi, Cilegon, Pekalongan are not targeted unless actual service coverage is explicitly expanded.

### Unrelated / unverified offer
batako and unrelated construction-material categories are not added unless the business confirms that offering.

## Canonical intent ownership

- Homepage: factory, producer, supplier, direct procurement, Jabodetabek.
- Products hub: sell/buy/catalog intent.
- Price page: broad commercial price intent.
- Installation page: installer/contractor/turnkey intent.
- Regional pages: location + commercial service combinations.
- Product detail pages: model/specification/application intent.
- Blog: informational and supporting price methodology.
- GBP + NAP + regional pages: near-me/terdekat intent.

## Anti-cannibalization rule

Do not create a separate thin page for every query variant. Cikarang is covered under Bekasi; Jakarta sub-city terms are covered on the Jakarta page; generic price terms consolidate on /harga-paving-block.
